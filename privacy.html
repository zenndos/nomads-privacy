Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Nomads Global ("we", "us", or "our") operates internal marketing research tools that use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) platform APIs. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Our application accesses publicly available business profile information through the Instagram Business Discovery API, including:

  • Business account username and biography
  • Follower count and media count
  • Recent post timestamps, like counts, and comment counts
  • Website URL listed on the business profile

We do not collect personal data from individual users, private messages, stories, or non-public content. We do not access any data that is not already publicly visible on Instagram business profiles.

2. How We Use the Data

The data we collect is used solely for:

  • Internal marketing research and competitive analysis
  • Understanding social media presence of businesses in our market
  • Generating aggregated, non-personally-identifiable reports

We do not sell, rent, or share raw data with third parties.

3. Data Retention

Collected data is stored in internal research databases and retained for up to 12 months. Data may be deleted earlier upon request or when it is no longer needed for our research purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties. Aggregated, anonymized insights may be used in internal reports. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than those described above.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about what data we hold related to your business profile
  • Request deletion of any data we have collected about your business
  • Object to our processing of your business profile data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@nomadsgl.com.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data we collect, including encrypted storage and access controls limited to authorized personnel.

7. Meta Platform Data

Our use of data received from Meta platform APIs complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policies. We only request permissions necessary for our stated purposes and do not use the data in ways that violate Meta's policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
